About Favor Makeup

Favor Makeup is a beauty business based in Sandton, Johannesburg, offering professional makeup and beauty services to clients for different occasions.

The business was founded by Rose Mashatane, a 28-year-old University of Johannesburg Engineering graduate who developed a passion for makeup and beauty.

Rose's passion for beauty inspired her to pursue a career in the makeup industry and grow Favour Makeup as a beauty brand.

Meet the Founder

Rose Mashatane

Rose Mashatane is the founder of Favor Makeup. Her background in engineering and her passion for makeup and beauty have contributed to her journey as an entrepreneur in the beauty industry.

Our Vision

Favor Makeup aims to grow further in the beauty industry and eventually develop and offer its own range of beauty products.
